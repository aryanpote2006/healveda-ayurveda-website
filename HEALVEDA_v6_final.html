The Three Doshas

Know Your Constitution

Ayurveda recognizes three fundamental bio-energies governing all physical and mental processes

🌬️
Vata
Air · Ether

Creative, quick-thinking, and energetic when balanced. Governs all movement — breath, circulation, nerve impulses, and elimination. The force of change.

Dry Light Cold Mobile Subtle
🔥
Pitta
Fire · Water

Ambitious, focused, and passionate when balanced. Rules all transformation — digestion, metabolism, body temperature, intelligence, and perception.

Hot Sharp Oily Intense Light
🌊
Kapha
Earth · Water

Calm, grounded, and nurturing when balanced. Provides structure, lubrication, and immunity. The principle of cohesion, stability, and nourishment.

Heavy Slow Cool Stable Smooth
